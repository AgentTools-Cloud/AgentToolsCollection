1. What this is

agent-tools.cloud is a free, open discovery directory of x402 paid services, MCP servers and A2A agents. We index public metadata so that AI agents and developers can find and evaluate callable services. We are a catalogue only — we do not host, operate, broker, or take payment for any third-party service listed here.

Listings are gathered by automated crawling and best-effort health checks; we do not guarantee that any entry is accurate, current, available, secure, or fit for any purpose. Our safety scan is a heuristic advisory signal, not a security guarantee. You are responsible for independently verifying any service before relying on or connecting to it.

Services listed in the directory are operated by independent third parties under their own terms. Any payment, data exchange, or interaction happens directly between you and that third party. We are not a party to those transactions and are not liable for them. We host neither the payer nor the payee side, so spending limits, budgets and approvals are set in your own wallet or client, not by us. The optional paid verification is a one-off test call capped at $1.00, not a payment service. Inclusion in the directory is not an endorsement.

You may search, query, and use the directory and its public API and MCP endpoints freely, including from automated agents. Please do not attempt to disrupt the service, abuse it to attack listed third parties, or scrape it at a rate that degrades availability for others. We may rate-limit or block clients that do. API keys are handed out without a gate because a key on its own grants no rights; do not use one to present yourself as the operator of a domain you do not control.

5. Listings & removal

Entries are sourced from public registries, public crawls, and submissions. If you operate a listed service you can correct it yourself by proving you control the domain that serves it — in a browser or over the API, see /docs/claim. Measured values such as availability and grade are not editable by anyone, including us through that path. You can also contact us and we will action reasonable requests, including removal.

Submit each service once, by its main endpoint. We read the other paid endpoints it advertises (for example in /.well-known/x402) and list them under that one entry, so there is no need to submit them separately. Submissions are limited per client, and that limit is part of the service: do not rotate IP addresses, accounts, keys or contact addresses to get past it, and do not split one origin into many separate submissions. We may merge such entries into one, reject further submissions for that origin or contact, and stop sending confirmation emails for them.
